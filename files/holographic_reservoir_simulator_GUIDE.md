### 1.2 Install heat-set inserts into chassis base
**Install thirty M3 brass heat-set inserts into the Chassis Lower Base.**
1. Locate the pre-drilled or CNC-routed mounting holes on the top surface of the Chassis Lower Base.
2. Clean out any wood dust or debris from the thirty mounting holes using a vacuum or compressed air.
3. Heat a soldering iron fitted with an M3-compatible brass insertion tip to 220°C.
4. Place an M3 Threaded Heat-Set Insert vertically over one of the chassis mounting holes.
5. Gently press the hot soldering iron tip into the center of the brass insert until it melts and sinks flush into the Chassis Lower Base.
6. Repeat the insertion process for all remaining twenty-nine mounting holes on the chassis base, ensuring each insert sits perfectly square.
  > Tip: Keep the soldering iron completely perpendicular to the MDF board to prevent the brass inserts from setting at an angle, which could cause misalignment during bracket assembly.
